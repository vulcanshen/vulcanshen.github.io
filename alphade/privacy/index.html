Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Alphade collects no data. Your videos stay on your Mac.

What Alphade collects

Nothing. Alphade has no server and no account, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Where your data stays

Alphade reads only the videos you open and writes only the videos you export, all on your Mac. While a video is being analyzed, Alphade keeps a temporary copy of the boxed area in its own temporary folder and deletes it when it's no longer needed. Nothing is uploaded.

Third parties

  • Apple in-app purchase: purchases are processed by Apple. Alphade never sees your payment details or your Apple Account; it only checks on your Mac whether the purchase has been made.
  • Crash reports: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may send the developer crash reports. They don't contain your videos.

Children

Alphade collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 12 日

Alphade 不收集任何資料，你的影片只留在你的 Mac 上。

Alphade 收集哪些資料

完全不收集。Alphade 沒有伺服器、沒有帳號，也沒有任何數據分析、廣告或追蹤。

資料存在哪裡

Alphade 只讀取你打開的影片、只寫出你輸出的影片，全部都在你的 Mac 上進行。分析影片時，Alphade 會在自己的暫存資料夾裡暫存框起來的那一塊區域，用完就刪除。不會上傳任何東西。

第三方

  • Apple 內購：購買由 Apple 處理。Alphade 看不到你的付款資料或 Apple 帳號，只會在你的 Mac 上確認是否已經購買。
  • 當機報告：如果你在 macOS 設定裡選擇與 app 開發者分享分析資料，Apple 可能會把當機報告提供給開發者。當機報告不包含你的影片。

兒童

Alphade 不收集任何人的資料，兒童也一樣。

政策變更
