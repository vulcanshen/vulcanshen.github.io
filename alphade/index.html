How it works

Draw a box. Alphade does the rest.

  1. Mark the watermark

    Open a video and drag a box around the watermark.

  2. Analyze

    Alphade studies the whole video and tells you how well it can restore it.

  3. Export

    Save a clean copy in H.264 or HEVC, at full size or smaller.

Before you export

Check every frame.

Compare the original and the result side by side, step through frame by frame and zoom in to 4×. Only the boxed area changes; the rest of the picture and the sound stay exactly as they were.
