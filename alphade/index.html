Video watermark remover for Mac

Restore what's under the logo.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Alphade removes semi-transparent watermarks, logos and text overlays from your videos, and brings back the picture underneath instead of blurring it away. Draw a box around the overlay: Alphade studies the whole video, works out how the overlay was blended in, and reverses it in every frame.

How it works

  • Open a video and drag a box around the watermark.
  • Click Analyze. Alphade checks every frame and tells you whether the watermark can be restored, can only be filled in, or isn't supported.
  • Step through the result frame by frame, compare it with the original, and loop the few seconds around any moment.
  • Export at the original size or downscale to 1080p or 720p, in H.264 or HEVC. Only the boxed area changes, and the audio is copied as is.

Private by design

Everything runs on your Mac. Your videos are never uploaded, there is no account to create, and Alphade collects no data.

Try it free

Opening, analyzing and previewing are free. Exporting needs a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later. Works best with semi-transparent watermarks that stay in the same place throughout the video; see which watermarks are supported.

Mac 上的影片浮水印去除工具

還原浮水印底下的畫面。

即將在 Mac App Store 推出

Alphade 能去除影片上的半透明浮水印、logo 與文字疊圖，而且是把底下原本的畫面還原回來，不是把它糊掉。在浮水印周圍拉一個框，Alphade 會分析整支影片，算出浮水印是怎麼疊上去的，再逐格反推回原本的畫面。

使用方式

  • 開啟影片，在浮水印周圍拉一個框。
  • 按「分析」。Alphade 會檢查每一格，告訴你這個浮水印「可還原」、「只能填補」還是「不支援」。
  • 逐格檢視結果、和原始畫面對照，也能循環播放任一位置前後幾秒。
  • 輸出時可保留原始解析度，或縮小到 1080p、720p，格式可選 H.264 或 HEVC。只有框起來的區域會改變，音訊原樣保留。

完全在你的 Mac 上處理

影片不會上傳，不需要註冊帳號，Alphade 也不收集任何資料。

免費試用

開啟、分析與預覽都免費。輸出需要一次買斷，不是訂閱。

系統需求

macOS 26 或更新版本。最適合位置固定、整支影片都在同一處的半透明浮水印；詳見哪些浮水印可以去除。
