Support

Restore what's under the logo.

Alphade removes semi-transparent watermarks, logos and text overlays from your videos on your Mac, and brings back the picture underneath instead of blurring it away.

Contact

Email vulcan.shen.2304@gmail.com. If something doesn't work, please include your macOS version, the video's format and size (for example MP4, 1920 × 1080), and a screenshot if you can.

Which watermarks can Alphade remove?

Semi-transparent watermarks, logos and text that stay in the same place for the whole video. Opaque logos can be filled in when the background behind them is simple, such as a solid color, a gradient or a slide. Watermarks that move, and opaque watermarks over detailed or live-action backgrounds, aren't supported.

What do Restorable, Fill Only and Unsupported mean?

  • Restorable: Alphade worked out how the watermark was blended in and recovers the original picture.
  • Fill Only: the watermark is opaque, so the area is filled in from the surrounding background. What was under it can't be recovered.
  • Unsupported: the area has to be filled in over a detailed background, so the result will look blurry.

Why can't my video be opened?

Alphade opens 8-bit (SDR) MP4, MOV and M4V videos. 10-bit and HDR videos, such as many iPhone recordings with HDR turned on, aren't supported yet.

How do I change the language?

Alphade follows your Mac's preferred languages and is available in English and Traditional Chinese. To use a different language for Alphade only, open System Settings › General › Language & Region, click + under Applications, choose Alphade and pick a language.

How do I restore my purchase?

Choose Alphade › Restore Purchases, or click Restore Purchases in the purchase window. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Alphade upload my videos?

No. Everything happens on your Mac, and Alphade collects no data. See the privacy policy.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later.

還原浮水印底下的畫面。

Alphade 在你的 Mac 上去除影片裡的半透明浮水印、logo 與文字疊圖，而且是把底下原本的畫面還原回來，不是把它糊掉。

聯絡

請寄信到 vulcan.shen.2304@gmail.com。如果遇到問題，請附上 macOS 版本、影片的格式與尺寸（例如 MP4、1920 × 1080），方便的話再附一張截圖。

哪些浮水印可以去除？

整支影片都固定在同一個位置的半透明浮水印、logo 與文字。不透明的 logo 如果背景很單純（純色、漸層、簡報畫面），可以用周圍的背景填補。會移動的浮水印，以及疊在細節豐富或實拍畫面上的不透明浮水印，目前不支援。

「可還原」、「只能填補」、「不支援」是什麼意思？

  • 可還原：Alphade 算出了浮水印是怎麼疊上去的，可以把原本的畫面還原回來。
  • 只能填補：浮水印是不透明的，只能用周圍的背景填補，底下原本的內容無法還原。
  • 不支援：需要在細節較多的背景上填補，結果會看起來模糊。

為什麼影片打不開？

Alphade 支援 8-bit（SDR）的 MP4、MOV、M4V 影片。10-bit 與 HDR 影片目前還不支援，例如開啟 HDR 錄影的 iPhone 影片。

怎麼切換語言？

Alphade 會跟著 Mac 的偏好語言顯示，支援英文與繁體中文。如果只想讓 Alphade 使用其他語言，請打開「系統設定 › 一般 › 語言與地區」，在「App」下方按「+」，選擇 Alphade 後再選語言。

怎麼恢復購買？

從選單選「Alphade › 恢復購買」，或在購買視窗按「恢復購買」。請使用當初購買時的 Apple 帳號。

怎麼申請退款？

退款由 Apple 處理，請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

Alphade 會上傳我的影片嗎？

不會。所有處理都在你的 Mac 上完成，Alphade 也不收集任何資料。詳見隱私權政策。
