Support

Be found without a network.

When a disaster takes the network down, HelpKnock turns each phone into a Bluetooth beacon: a short-range rescue app that helps trapped people be found, be identified and talk with rescuers nearby.

Not a replacement for emergency services. If you have any signal, call 119 or your local emergency number first.

Contact

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue at github.com/vulcanshen/vulcanshen.github.io/issues and start the title with [HelpKnock]. A GitHub account is needed.

No GitHub account? Email vulcan.shen.2304@gmail.com.

Getting started

Fill in your details, choose what to share, and allow Bluetooth, Location and Motion & Fitness while you are calm. When something happens, you only have to open the app and tap “I need help”.

Good to know

  • Both phones need HelpKnock.
  • Bluetooth reaches only a short distance, and less through walls and rubble.
  • Do not swipe HelpKnock away in the app switcher: iOS will not reopen it, and nobody can find you. After a restart, open it again.

Privacy

HelpKnock has no server and no account. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.6 or later, and Bluetooth turned on.

開始使用

趁平常把資料填好、選好要分享什麼，並允許藍牙、定位與「動作與健身」。真的出事時，只要打開 app、點「我需要幫助」。

要知道的事

  • 雙方的手機都要裝 HelpKnock。
  • 藍牙只傳得到很短的距離，隔牆、隔瓦礫會更短。
  • 不要在多工畫面把 HelpKnock 滑掉：iOS 不會再把它打開，別人就找不到你。重開機後要再打開一次。

隱私

HelpKnock 沒有伺服器、沒有帳號。請看隱私權政策。

系統需求

iOS 18.6 或更新版本的 iPhone 或 iPad，並且開著藍牙。
