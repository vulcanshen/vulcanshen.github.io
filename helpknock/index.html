Be found without a network.

When a disaster takes the network down, your phone becomes a Bluetooth beacon: rescuers nearby can find you, see how you are, and message you. No signal needed.

Free · iPhone and iPad · iOS 18.6 or later

Not a replacement for emergency services. If you have any signal, call your local emergency number first.

How it works

Three steps, and only one of them in an emergency.

  1. 1

    Get ready today

    Fill in your details, choose what to share, and allow Bluetooth, Location, Motion & Fitness and Local Network while you are calm.

  2. 2

    Tap “I need help”

    Your phone starts broadcasting at once and keeps going with the screen locked. Nothing else to press, nothing to type.

  3. 3

    Rescuers find you

    Rescuers nearby see your battery, how long the phone has been still and your last known position, and can message you.

For people who need help

One tap, then it keeps working for you.

  • Battery, time since you last touched the phone and your last position are shared automatically.
  • Report fire, flooding, live wires, gas or injuries with one switch each, one hand.
  • See how many rescuers are nearby and whether one has read your status, and message them.
  • While the app is open, your status and messages also reach rescuers on the same Wi-Fi. With the screen locked, Wi-Fi stops but Bluetooth keeps broadcasting.
  • Check the unit and skills a rescuer entered, and report or block anyone who seems wrong.

For rescuers

See who needs help first.

  • One card per person: battery, idle and still time, hazards and headcount at a glance.
  • A radar of the beacons you can hear, plus people only other rescuers can hear.
  • Mark “I am handling this”, and every rescuer in range sees it.
  • Teams with a roster and a team chat, no network needed.
  • While the app is open, rescuers on the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot also share who they hear, marks and messages, no internet needed.

Messages

Messages that travel phone to phone.

  • Talk with rescuers over Bluetooth, with no signal at all.
  • A message to someone out of your range is passed on by rescuers who can hear them.
  • See when a message has been delivered and read.

Privacy

No server. No account.

Your personal and medical details stay in your phone's keychain, and you choose what to share when you ask for help. The developer never receives anything you enter.

Anyone nearby or on the same Wi-Fi in the rescuer role can read what you share, none of it is verified, and Bluetooth and Wi-Fi transfers are not encrypted in this version. Read the privacy policy.
