Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Ciscrux collects no data and never connects to the internet.

What Ciscrux collects

Nothing. Ciscrux has no network code, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't collect any information about you or your Mac.

Where your data stays

The only thing Ciscrux keeps is its options: the line thickness, which figures to show, Show Hint, the layout, the animation and the three colors. macOS saves them on your Mac with the screen saver's settings, and they never leave it. The moon phase is worked out on your Mac from the date.

Third parties

None.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
